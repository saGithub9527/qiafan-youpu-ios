1. Information We Process and Why

1. Startup, Login, and Sessions

Startup configuration and network requests carry a random device identifier generated by this app, the client platform, version, and applicable language information. When processing a request, the server also encounters network connection information such as the IP address. Protected requests carry a session credential to verify access rights. The device identifier is not a hardware serial number. Startup configuration requests occur before login, so network processing does not take place only after login.

Quick Login uses a random login credential in the device's secure storage to establish a system account and session and processes the associated login time, authorization status, and session updates. The server validates a credential digest or equivalent, while secure storage on the client holds the secret credential required for login. Device credentials and complete tokens are not public account information; do not share them.

When you actively use Sign in with Apple, the system displays the authorization page, and the server verifies the authorization result and associates the Apple user identifier. This version does not request Apple's name or email scopes and does not obtain your Apple password. Apple's own processing is described in Sign in with Apple & Privacy. Revoking Apple authorization does not delete data already saved by this app.

2. Profile, Settings, and Interests

Profile information includes your nickname, optional country code, region, biography, avatar, system ID, and any account information actually bound to the account. A saved avatar is displayed publicly. Post details display the author's nickname, public user ID, and any country, region, and avatar provided; the internal database ID is not used as a public identity. Do not place information in your public profile that you do not want others to see.

The app language and theme are stored only on the device. The server uses the language code carried by each request to return content in that language and does not store the app language in the account profile. Notification preferences, including plan reminders and post or image updates, are stored with the account. Interests include the countries, cuisines, tastes, spice level, and excluded ingredients you select and are used to save your food content preferences. Saving a preference does not mean that every page currently provides automatic recommendations or that a health diagnosis is being created. You do not need to explain your medical history, religious beliefs, or why you do not eat a particular ingredient. Optional items can be changed or cleared on the corresponding page.

3. Favorites, Posts, and Drafts

Favorites process recipe or post identifiers and the favorite relationship so that favorites can be queried or removed. They are saved privately by account.

Posts process a title, description, images, optional video, ingredient names and units, steps, an optional linked recipe, an optional cover image, draft or published status, author, and creation and update times. A private draft can be accessed only by its owner in the app. After you actively publish it successfully, the post and its referenced media can be read by other logged-in users. Editing a published post is still an edit to public content and does not automatically convert it into a private draft.

4. Cooking, Purchases, and Plans

One cooking record corresponds to one dish and includes the date you actually record, dish name, meal period, currency, optional cost for that dish, ingredients and quantity units, and notes. It is used for the monthly calendar, number of cooking days, and related statistics. A day may include multiple meal periods, and a meal period may include multiple dishes; each dish stores its own ingredients and cost. A missing cost remains empty and is not treated as zero.

Purchase and inventory data include the merchant, purchase or acquisition date, time and time zone, ingredient names, quantities, units, package specifications, storage locations, expiry dates, currencies, prices, discounts, refunds and returns. Confirming a new receipt saves both the receipt and its inventory. Actual cooking quantities reduce inventory and determine consumption cost. Purchase spending and consumption cost are reported separately; unknown prices are not zero. We do not convert currencies automatically or read payment accounts. Posted receipts can be corrected, refunded or returned under the page rules; accounting entries are retained. Removing an inventory display does not delete the original receipt or accounting entries.

Adding a plan processes the recipes, dates, meals and servings you select. Personal data are separated by account. If you choose a household scope, its plans, purchases, inventory and associated cooking costs are available to current authorized household members according to their permissions. Adding a plan does not create a completed cooking record; actual records and quantities are submitted when you confirm completion. Favorites remain private to your account.

5. Image Contributions, Feedback, and Rights Complaints

An image contribution processes the ingredient or recipe you select, the uploaded image, submission time, processing status and reason, and any withdrawal request so that the image and corresponding entry can be reviewed. Records for which an image license was separately confirmed in the past additionally retain the confirmation status and version of the terms that applied at that time. A submission without separate confirmation does not generate a confirmed marker, and its submission time is not treated as a separate license confirmation time. The scope of image use and withdrawal method are described in the Image License Agreement. A pending image does not automatically become a public catalog image when submitted. An accepted image that is still referenced by the catalog can be read by other logged-in users. Staff with administrator permission can view pending images in the review interface and leave necessary operation records.

Help and Feedback processes the category you select—issue report, feature suggestion, account issue, or other—the submitted text, and processing records. Do not include unnecessary contact details or sensitive information in the feedback text. A rights complaint processes the location of the content involved, description, contact details, electronic signature, good-faith and authorization declarations, creation time, status, and processing notes. Provide only information needed to locate the issue and establish your rights; do not submit complete tokens, unrelated identity documents, medical records, or another person's private information. Complaint text, signatures, and contact details are not available for public lookup by ordinary users. The circumstances in which material must be provided to a relevant party to investigate a dispute are described in the Image Rights Protection Rules.

6. Search and Service Operations

A catalog or favorites search sends the keywords you enter to the current service to return matching content. To address faults and security incidents, the app and server process operational information such as request IDs, API paths, response status, and duration. Network connection and error records may also include the IP address and content you actively submit in a request. We restrict access according to operational security and troubleshooting needs and do not use logs for unrelated advertising or profiling.

2. Media Selection, File Contents, and System Permissions

When you choose an avatar, post image, video, or contribution image, the app uses the system photo picker and receives only files you expressly select. It does not obtain ongoing access to your entire photo library as a result. You may cancel the selection. Publication or contribution features that depend on an image require the necessary image; other features that do not depend on media remain available.

The current client redraws a selected image and encodes it as JPEG, limiting the longest side to 2,048 pixels, and does not copy original EXIF information. This does not remove faces, home addresses, or other information already visible in the image itself. A video is read and uploaded from the file you select. There is no uniform metadata-removal or transcoding process, so the video may retain original metadata such as sound, recording time, device, or location. Check the video file and its visible content before selecting it.

Avatars, post images or videos, and contribution images are first stored in the local draft directory after selection and are not uploaded merely because they were selected. The client begins uploading the relevant media only when you expressly save the profile, publish the post, or submit the image contribution. A submission may upload media before saving the business record. If that later save fails, an uploaded file may remain on the server as a private, unreferenced file belonging to the current account. New server-side uploads are initially held privately by the current account. Media enters the corresponding public read scope only after an avatar is saved, a post passes review and is published, or a contributed image is actually accepted. Media is currently stored in the business database. A byte digest identifies identical, not-yet-public uploads by the same account to avoid duplicate quota use during retries; files are not merged across accounts.

Opening the receipt scanner requests camera permission to read QR codes on your device. QR images selected through the system photo picker are also decoded locally. Scanning does not upload camera frames or selected QR images, or automatically open addresses in QR codes. A merchant receipt detail service is not connected; you can proceed to manual entry after detection. Without camera permission, you can still select a photo or add a receipt manually. These functions do not request microphone, location or contacts permissions.

The notification switch records your reminder preference; it does not mean that the operating system has granted notification permission. The app starts the Getui SDK and requests and registers system notifications only after you have accepted this Policy, signed in, and enabled plan reminders. You can turn off plan reminders in the app or notifications in device settings. Signing out disables the push registration for the current device. A small number of messages that had already entered the operating-system or provider delivery path when you disabled it may still arrive afterward.

3. On-Device Storage and Diagnostic Records

The theme and some interface state are stored on the device. To reduce repeated loading, images and network responses may be cached on the device; you can clear these re-downloadable caches in Settings. Clearing a cache does not delete the account, favorites, saved content, or unfinished drafts. Login credentials use the system's secure storage. Ordinary “Clear Cache” and logout do not cancel the account, and secure system storage is not guaranteed to be fully removed when the app is uninstalled.

Local drafts include unfinished profile, preference, post, plan, cooking, purchase, inventory, image contribution and complaint forms. They may contain text, preferences, contact details, media identifiers, dates, meals, ingredients, quantities, specifications and costs. Unsubmitted forms and media remain on the device until you confirm submission; media already uploaded during submission are stored by the server. Drafts are separated by service environment, account and applicable personal or household scope. Access after saving is described in Section 1. Drafts use application storage rather than a vault encrypted with an individual account key. Protect your device and system backups.

Drafts remain after logout so the same account can continue editing. The applicable local draft is removed after successful submission or when you expressly discard it on the corresponding page. Autosaving a cooking draft or changing its date or meal period does not mean it has been submitted and does not independently write to the server. Clearing an image or network cache does not also clear drafts. Deleting a local draft does not automatically delete a file already uploaded to the server, and a server draft and the local editing copy are not the same record.

The publicly distributed version uses PLCrashReporter to create crash records locally and does not upload them automatically. A crash record may contain the device and system environment, app version, threads and call stacks, crash time, and process information. The archive rotates at a maximum of 20 records or 20 MiB by count and storage limits; this limit is not a fixed number of retention days. A recipient receives the file only if you actively provide the log for troubleshooting.

An internal debug version additionally has on-device request logs that may contain original request headers, parameters, responses, account information, complete session tokens, or device secret credentials. It retains at most 500 records or 10 MiB, with further truncation limits on content. The publicly distributed version does not include this request-log implementation or entry. Do not publicly share internal debug logs; review any material before providing it for necessary troubleshooting.

This app uses Alamofire for network requests, Kingfisher for the image cache, SnapKit for layout, and PLCrashReporter for on-device crash records. MBProgressHUD displays loading progress, and MJRefresh displays the state of loading more items in a list. The last two are used under the MIT License and do not themselves connect to a network or send your information to their maintainers. Integration does not give these components independent permission to receive data. Network data is sent to the actual service recipients for the purposes described in this Policy.

5. Retention, Deletion, and Withdrawal

Retention must be limited to the time necessary to fulfill a stated purpose: account and session data maintains and verifies login; posts, drafts, and other records support the storage and management you choose; feedback, license, and complaint records support investigation, handling, and necessary proof of rights. The specific end condition should take account of your deletion or withdrawal, completion of an account request, whether a dispute has ended, and applicable statutory retention requirements. Retention may not be extended arbitrarily.

The current pages allow you to remove a favorite and delete your own post, cooking record, or purchase record. Deleting a post removes the post, its public media references, and favorite relationships. Changing the avatar changes the corresponding public avatar reference. These operations do not mean that the underlying uploaded file has been physically deleted, and the same file may remain readable if it is still used in other published content.

A withdrawn pending contribution retains a withdrawn status. For an accepted contribution, “Request Withdrawal” submits a request only and does not mean the image has stopped being displayed before replacement or takedown is complete. Uploaded but unused media and old media replaced by a newer file currently have no automatic expiration and deletion process and may remain on the server and count against the account's media quota. Account, contribution, complaint, and operational records are managed according to the service purpose, request handling, and necessary statutory retention. Backup copies do not disappear at the same time as a page operation. If you request deletion, we will explain what can be deleted, what must be retained by law, and what has been completed; we will not describe a page status change as the immediate and complete deletion of every copy.

You may request deletion or restriction of processing through the channel in Section 7. If information must be retained by law or cannot temporarily be deleted for technical reasons, the reason should be explained and the information restricted to necessary storage and security protection. It will no longer be used for activities unrelated to the original purpose. Methods for retaining and removing local drafts and diagnostic files appear in Section 3.
