3. Profile, Preferences, and Private Content

Provide profile information and preferences according to the purpose shown on the page, and do not impersonate another person. Preferences such as country, cuisine, taste, spice level, and ingredients you do not eat are used for the product features you select. You are not required to provide medical records, religious beliefs, or other unrelated sensitive information. Optional information may be left blank.

Post drafts save unpublished content. A draft enters the relevant public display scope only after you actively choose to publish it and receive a successful result. We do not automatically convert a private draft into a public post. A notice that an upload or save is in progress or has failed does not constitute successful publication.

Favorites, cooking records, purchase bills, and plans are saved privately by account. One cooking record corresponds to one dish. A day may contain multiple meal periods, and a meal period may contain multiple dishes; ingredients and optional costs are recorded separately for each dish. Cooking costs and purchase expenses are separate records and are calculated according to the currencies and amounts you actually enter. A missing cooking cost is not treated as zero. The service does not automatically convert currencies or deduct inventory. Selecting “Add to Plan” does not mean the dish has been cooked or that a purchase has occurred. Local sample figures must not be treated as actual records.

To make it easier to continue editing, the profile, preferences, posts, cooking, image contribution, and complaint pages may save unfinished forms on the device, separated by account and service environment; these drafts remain after logout. The dishes, meal periods, ingredients, currencies, and costs in a cooking draft remain only on the device and are sent to the server to create a cooking record only when you expressly submit them. Autosaving or changing the date or meal period does not constitute submission. The same account can continue editing on a later visit, and the local copy is removed after successful submission or when you expressly discard that draft. Avatars, post images or videos, and contribution images are first stored in the local draft directory after selection. Uploading begins only when you expressly save the profile, publish the post, or submit the image contribution. A submission may upload media before saving the business record. If that later save fails, an uploaded file may remain on the server as a private, unreferenced file belonging to the current account. Canceling editing or deleting a local draft does not mean that file has been deleted from the server. See the Privacy Policy for details.
